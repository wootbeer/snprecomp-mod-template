## Health Regen

While you're hurt, your health slowly fills back up.

### How to install
1. Download `snp_health_regen.nrm` below.
2. Open the Sin and Punishment recomp and go to the Mods tab.
3. Drop the `.nrm` file in and turn the mod on.

You can change the speed in the mod's options (Regen Speed). Lower is faster.

Needs the Sin and Punishment recomp 0.2.7 or newer.
